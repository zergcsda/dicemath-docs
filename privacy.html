Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Data Not Collected

Dice Math 3D does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.

The short version

No accounts or sign-in.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

The app works fully offline and does not send anything over the internet.

Information stored on your device

To make practice work, the app keeps your settings (grade, dice count, operators, language), your score history and your Mistakes Book on your device only.

The Apple Watch app keeps its own settings, history and Mistakes Book on the watch. They are not synced with your iPhone or sent anywhere else.

We cannot see this information. It is deleted when you delete the app. Like other app data, it may be included in device backups that you make with Apple, which are covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

You can clear the history at any time in Settings → History.

Device features

Motion sensor: used only to notice when you shake the device to roll the dice. Motion readings are processed in real time and never saved.

Read aloud: questions are spoken with Apple’s built-in, on-device speech. No audio is recorded and the microphone is never used.

Haptics: used for taps and feedback only.

Children’s privacy

Dice Math 3D is made for children. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children.

Settings and the support email link are behind a parental gate, so children cannot leave the app or change settings without an adult.

Purchases

Dice Math 3D is a paid app with no in-app purchases. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we do not receive your payment details.

If you email us

If you contact us at support@keepsub.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. Emails sent from the app’s “Contact Support” link are prefilled with the app version, device model and iOS version, which you can edit or remove before sending.

We use this only to answer you, and we delete it on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact
